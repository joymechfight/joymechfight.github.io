FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Here you can read through some frequently asked questions about both this game, and this website!


ABOUT JOY MECH FIGHT

"What is this game?"

Joy Mech Fight (JMF) is a 2D fighting game developed and published by Nintendo for the Famicom in 1993, notable for being one of the few traditional fighting games on the platform. Despite its Japan-only release, Joy Mech Fight gained a cult following for its innovative animation and fun gameplay, and is considered one of the best fighting games on the Famicom. The game features a roster of 36 characters, made up of several floating limbs which allow the game to create a variety of animations by simply moving the sprites around rather than having to make new ones. It's great for anyone looking for a fun, fast game that's easy to get into; there's a bit of weird kusoge jankiness, and it's not the most balanced experience, but all things considered, it's aged better than you might expect.


"What is CE?"

Joy Mech Fight: Community Edition is a mod created by RefreshingLemonade (RL), and is the standard version used in tournaments. The mod removes Leo, a banned character who in vanilla can effectively convert into a ToD off any stray hit, and removes Houou's Crash special, making him a viable character for competitive play. That aside, it also adds a 5-minute timer, something which the original game lacked, alongside changing certain robots' names to more accurately reflect their original Japanese ones.


ABOUT REPLAY ARCHIVE

"What is this website?"

Joy Mech Fight Replay Archive (JMFRA) is a website created by Green, with the purpose of documenting, archiving, and preserving information related to Joy Mech Fight and its competitive scene.


"What do the colors on players' names mean?"

A player's name is colored yellow if they have not entered a tournament within the last 6 months, but are still active in the community. A player's name is colored red if they have not been active within the community for a year or more.


"What do the different ranks mean?"

Gold Rank is given to those who have advanced knowledge of the game's mechanics, and have entered at least one tournament.
Platinum Rank is given to those who have expertize knowledge of the game's mechanics, and have gotten top 3 out of a 7 or more entree tournament.
Master Rank is given to those who have nearly complete knowledge of the game's mechanics, and have won a 7 or more entree tournament.


"Why is Skater the favicon?"

Skater was chosen as the favicon of the website because he is such a terrible and unpopular character, that if he wasn't included in the favicon, he would appear nowhere else in the website other than pages directly related to him, as nobody plays as, researches, or cares about him in any way.
